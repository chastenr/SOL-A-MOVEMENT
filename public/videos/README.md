# Veora hero video assets

The homepage hero uses a custom 12.52-second montage assembled from four
Pexels clips. Pexels permits free website use and modification under its
license: https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/

## Included sequence

1. Pilates equipment — Pexels video 29474205
   https://www.pexels.com/video/dynamic-pilates-workout-on-professional-equipment-29474205/
2. Mat movement — Pexels video 6651033
   https://www.pexels.com/video/woman-stretching-over-a-mat-6651033/
3. Yoga — Pexels video 6448032
   https://www.pexels.com/video/a-woman-meditating-while-sitting-on-a-yoga-mat-6448032/
4. Barre / ballet — Pexels video 5147629 by Budgeron Bach
   https://www.pexels.com/video/beautiful-asian-girl-doing-dance-rehearsal-in-studio-5147629/

Every featured participant is Asian. The clips are trimmed to 3.5 seconds
each, joined with restrained 0.5-second cross-dissolves, muted, and given a
consistent warm-neutral grade. No stock audio, logos, or baked-in text are
included. Portrait footage uses a purpose-built desktop composition and a
separate mobile crop, keeping faces visible at both aspect ratios.

## Production exports

- `hero-loop.mp4` — 1920×1080 H.264, 25 fps, 12.52 seconds, approximately 2.7 MB.
- `hero-loop-mobile.mp4` — manually cropped 720×1280 H.264, 25 fps,
  12.52 seconds, approximately 1.3 MB.
- `hero-poster.webp` — matching 1920×1080 desktop poster.
- `hero-poster-mobile.webp` — matching 720×1280 mobile poster.

The website heading and calls to action remain accessible HTML over the video.
The video is muted, looping, inline, control-free, and uses separate mobile and
desktop sources.

## Future client-footage upgrade

These stock clips are a polished launch-ready solution, but they do not depict
the actual Veora studio or instructors. When original Veora footage is
available, replace the four shots with 4K clips of the real studio, ideally
showing primarily Asian / Southeast Asian adult women with enough space for
both landscape and portrait crops. The existing edit timing and file paths can
remain unchanged.
